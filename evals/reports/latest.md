### Every clean case that did not pass untouched

- **clean-expr-repeat-over-fares** (withheld) — json-render's own iteration: one card template repeated over the loaded fares, with props reading $item. This is how a renderer is meant to be used, and core blocks it today because an $item prop cannot be attributed to a single record. Expected to fail, and here to say by how much.
  - "card" was withheld and should have rendered
  - guard said: requireBinding: FlightCard.airline is an expression, and the guard cannot attribute its result to a record in fares. Bind it directly to a field instead.
  - guard said: requireBinding: FlightCard.price is an expression, and the guard cannot attribute its result to a record in fares. Bind it directly to a field instead.
  - guard said: requireBinding: FlightCard.stops is an expression, and the guard cannot attribute its result to a record in fares. Bind it directly to a field instead.
